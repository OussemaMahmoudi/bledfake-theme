ABOUT
BLED FAKE

We don't hate Tunisia.
We hate the illusion.

BLED FAKE is an underground streetwear label born in the streets of Tunis. Not a brand. A document. Every garment is evidence.

01

THE NAME

Bled means homeland in Tunisian Arabic. Fake is the mirror we hold up to it. Not as an insult — as a diagnosis. We exist in the space between what Tunisia is and what it pretends to be. That gap is where underground culture is born.

02

THE GARMENTS

Each piece is designed in Tunis and produced in limited quantities. We use heavyweight organic cottons, technical ripstop, and screen-printed graphics with puff ink. Every drop tells a chapter of the same story.

03

THE COLLECTIONS

BLED FAKE — Confrontational graphics, oversized silhouettes.

BLED HORRA — Free Tunisia. Experimental cuts, raw fabrication.

COMEBACK TOMORROW — Tactical utility garments and accessories.

WE DON'T
HATE TUNISIA.

WE HATE THE ILLUSION.

This is not a brand story. This is a police report. A document of what happens when a generation refuses to pretend.
I
We grew up watching Tunisia sell itself to tourism and a narrative of stability, while the streets told a completely different story. Bled was supposed to mean home. It started to feel like a stage set.
II
"The garment is the argument.
The wearer is the proof."
III
We ship from Tunis. We pay Tunisian creatives, photographers, and seamstresses. The money stays. The culture stays. The illusion gets exposed.
This is the reality.
Enter it.
